Aaghaaz Tamasha is a blocky family-house game about the situations new couples really face: the wedding night, the aunty next door, who decides the budget, whose parents to visit. One house, one parrot, forty real situations. Play solo, or each play your own side with your partner.

Play in your browser Get the Android app

No account. No ads. No purchases. Works offline for solo play.

How it works

1. Pick a side

Play the bride or the groom, through 12 situations or the full journey of 40.

2. Read, tap, choose

Each card is a real moment. Tap your character and decide how you respond. No single answer pleases everyone.

3. Compare with your partner

Share a room code on WhatsApp and each play your own side of the same year. The point is not the score. It is seeing where you read the same moment differently, and talking about it.

Google asks every new developer to test with at least 12 real people for 14 days before an app can go on the Play Store. If you have an Android phone and 10 minutes across two weeks, you can help. Send us the Gmail address you use on your phone and we will add you to the test group.

A note on safety. The game is about ordinary conflict and learning to talk. It never treats abuse or control as something to adjust to. If you or someone you know is being hurt, threatened or controlled, please contact local emergency services or a trusted person. Aaghaaz Tamasha is for learning and conversation, not legal, medical, religious or counselling advice.
