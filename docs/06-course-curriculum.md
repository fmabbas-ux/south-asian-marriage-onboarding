# 06. Course Curriculum

Course title: **Pehla Saal** (the first year), under the **Dehleez** brand.

## Format
- **Languages:** Urdu/Hindi (primary spoken), English subtitles; English version for diaspora.
- **Tracks:** Bride track, Groom track, Couple track (watched together), Family track (short).
- **Lesson shape (8 to 12 minutes):** 1 scene (60 to 90 seconds, acted or animated) > 2 concept > 3 skill/script > 4 exercise > 5 takeaway card.
- **Release cadence:** Pre-marriage modules first (longest decision window), then months 1 to 12.
- **Companions:** workbook PDF, agreement templates, self-assessment, scenario game.

## Modules

| # | Module | Core lessons | Exercise |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wedding vs marriage | What changes on day one; the seven dynamics; how this course works | Write your "what I'm afraid of / excited about" list |
| 2 | Know yourself, know your partner | Family-of-origin script, attachment, stress style, money scripts | Expectations audit (50 questions, done separately, compared together) |
| 3 | The conversations before the wedding | Disclosure, deal-breakers, nikahnama, mehr, living arrangement, children, work | Pre-marriage agreements sheet |
| 4 | Communication that works | Soft start-up, I-statements, listening, taking breaks, repair | Role-play: 3 scripted arguments |
| 5 | The first 30 days | Routines, privacy, intimacy, exhaustion, the first fight | 30-day check-in plan |
| 6 | Parents | United front, honour vs obey, translating for each other | Script: "Ammi, here is what we decided" |
| 7 | Siblings and relatives | Information discipline, sibling support limits, the three standard answers | Practice the three answers |
| 8 | Money | Scripts, transparency, structure, support to parents | Joint budget and monthly money meeting |
| 9 | Ambition and growth | Work, study, relocation, five-year plan | Merged five-year plan |
| 10 | Intimacy and children | Talking first, consent, fertility timing, pressure | Couple conversation guide (separate then together) |
| 11 | Complex families | Step-children, previous marriage, second wife law, exes, health | Case studies with decision tree |
| 12 | Mental health and help | Low mood, anxiety, burnout; counselling; mediation | "When to get help" checklist |
| 13 | When it is not working | Early warning signs, repair, abuse recognition, separation options, the legal basics | Safety plan template |
| 14 | The first-year review | What worked, what to change, anniversary plan | Year-one review sheet |
| F | For families (short track) | Supporting the couple, mother-in-law role, bride's parents letting go | Family reflection card |

## Assessments
- Pre and post **self-rating** on 12 skills (communication, conflict, boundaries, money, intimacy conversations, etc.).
- **Couple agreement-gap score** from the game (how far the partners' answers differ on the same scenario).
- Follow-up survey at 3, 6 and 12 months of marriage.

## Production notes
- Casting: a mixed ensemble of actors; avoid caricatures of any ethnic, regional or religious group.
- Counsellor voice-over: a licensed Pakistani psychologist as the "expert" thread, plus a lawyer for legal lessons.
- Each lesson reviewed by one clinician and (for legal topics) one lawyer before release.
- Short clips (60 to 90 seconds) for free distribution on YouTube Shorts, Instagram and TikTok to feed the paid course.
