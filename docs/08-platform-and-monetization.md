# 08. Platform and Monetization

Verified 5 November 2026 unless marked **verify**. Platform rules change often: re-check before committing.

## 1. YouTube from Pakistan

- **YouTube Partner Program (YPP) tiers [sourced]:** expanded tier (fan funding, shopping early access) needs 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in 90 days, and either 3,000 watch hours in 12 months or 3 million Shorts views in 90 days. Full tier (ad revenue share) needs 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 watch hours or 10 million Shorts views. Source: [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240?hl=en-GB).
- **Pakistan status [sourced, partial]:** the expanded-YPP country list on that page does not include Pakistan. Whether Pakistan has standard ad-revenue YPP and channel memberships today is not confirmed by the pages read. **Verify** in YouTube Studio > Earn (the region selector) and Google AdSense's supported-country page.
- **Practical reading:** do not make the business depend on YouTube's own paywall features (memberships, paid "courses"). Use YouTube as the **free top-of-funnel** (Shorts, long-form teaching clips, community), and put the paid course on a website you control.

## 3. Taking payments from Pakistan
From a Pakistan-focused review (checked October 2026): [Paddle vs Lemon Squeezy for Pakistan founders](https://jawadhassan.dev/blog/paddle-vs-lemonsqueezy-pakistan).
- **Stripe:** not available directly to Pakistan-based businesses. Workarounds (US entity) add cost and compliance.
- **Paddle:** pays out by wire or Payoneer; $100 minimum; 5% + $0.50 per transaction; stricter onboarding.
- **Lemon Squeezy:** bank transfer or PayPal payouts; $50 minimum; 5% + $0.50 plus add-ons; its move to Stripe Managed Payments needs checking for Pakistan.
- **Local rails (Pakistan buyers):** JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank transfer (Raast), cards via a local gateway. **Verify** a local gateway that suits digital products, since most Pakistani buyers will not use USD cards.
- **Diaspora buyers:** Paddle or Lemon Squeezy in USD, GBP, CAD.
- **Tax and structure:** register a sole proprietorship or company as suits the volume; consult an accountant about FBR registration and digital-services rules.
