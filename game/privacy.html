Privacy policy: Aaghaaz Tamasha

A Dehleez project. Effective 11 November 2026. This policy covers the web game at aaghaaz-tamasha.vercel.app and the Android app that opens the same game.

The short version

You can play without an account, without giving your name and without any advertising or tracking. We only receive personal information if you choose to send us feedback and choose to add a contact detail.

What we collect, and why

WhatWhenWhy
Your feedback message, its category, and game details (version, language, screen, round, ending)Only when you press Send in the feedback box. The game details can be switched off with the tick box.To fix problems and improve the game.
A contact detail you type (email, WhatsApp number, phone number or social handle)Only if you choose to add one.Only to reply to you about that message.
Your internet address (IP)When feedback is sent, and in standard server logs kept by our hosting provider.To limit spam (a few messages per hour) and keep the service secure. We do not store it with your message.
Language choiceSaved on your own device.So the game opens in your language. It never leaves your device.

Your choices in the game, your scores and your private ledger are not sent to us and are not stored after you close the game.

Playing online with your partner

Online play connects your two devices directly using a room code. Your choices are sent to your partner's device so both games stay in step. A public connection service (PeerJS) helps the two devices find each other, and in doing so it and your partner's device can see your internet address. We do not record online games.

Services we rely on

  • Vercel hosts the game and processes feedback submissions.
  • GitHub stores feedback messages in a private tracker only the team can see.
  • Google Fonts supplies the Urdu typeface, and unpkg supplies the online-play library; both receive a standard web request from your device.

We do not sell your information, share it for advertising, or use analytics or advertising trackers.

How long we keep it

Feedback and any contact detail are kept while the matter is open and for up to 24 months afterwards, then deleted, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete anything you sent us. Open the feedback box in the game, choose the category Privacy, and quote your reference number (it starts with FB-) if you have it. We aim to answer privacy requests within 3 days and complete them within 30 days.

Children

This game is about marriage and is intended for adults (18 and over). It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Everything is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Feedback is stored in a private, access-controlled tracker.

Not professional advice

The game is for learning. It is not a substitute for counselling, legal or religious advice. If you are in danger, contact your local emergency services.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update the date above. To contact us about privacy, use the feedback box in the game and choose the category Privacy, or email hello@polymat-consulting.com. The game is published by PolyMat Studio, Pakistan.
