# 09. Roadmap

## Phase 1: Foundation (weeks 1 to 4)
- Complete research gaps listed in `01-research-summary.md` section 4.
- Finish competitive scan.
- Expand the 40 scenarios into full two-perspective texts (start with 12).
- Recruit reviewers: one Pakistani clinical psychologist or marriage counsellor, one family-law lawyer.

## Phase 2: Validate (weeks 5 to 8)
- Interview 15 to 20 people: recently married brides and grooms, mothers-in-law, counsellors, nikah registrars.
- Test 6 scenarios as text cards with 10 couples; record which resonate.
- Draft the expectations audit and run it with 5 engaged couples.

## Phase 3: MVP content (weeks 9 to 16)
- Script and produce Modules 1 to 4 (free YouTube set + paid core).
- Build the static web game with 12 scenarios, solo mode first.
- Launch landing page with email waitlist, YouTube channel with 20 Shorts.

## Phase 4: Paid launch (weeks 17 to 24)
- Set up payments (Paddle or Lemon Squeezy for USD, local gateway for PKR).
- Release full course Modules 1 to 14 in stages; Duo mode of the game.
- Partner with 5 wedding-ecosystem businesses and 2 counsellors.

## Phase 5: Scale
- Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Sinhala versions; diaspora version.
- Printed deck and workbook; gift bundles.
- Follow-up studies at 6 and 12 months; publish anonymised outcomes.

## Open decisions
1. Brand name and domain (check availability).
2. Religion framing: Islamic-values-aware first, or fully neutral?
3. Counsellor partners vs in-house.
4. Entity: sole proprietor vs company.
5. Open-source scope: what stays public (research, topic map) vs paid (scripts, game content).
