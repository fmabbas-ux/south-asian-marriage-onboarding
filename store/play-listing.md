## Full description (4000 max)
Aaghaaz Tamasha is a story game about the first year of a South Asian marriage. You play the bride or the groom in a blocky family house with Ammi-ji, Abbu-ji, a nand, a devar, an aunty next door, a cat and a parrot with opinions.

Each chapter is a real first-year situation: the wedding night, the phone that lights up, who cooks, whose family gets Eid, the salary question, the "good news" questions, and many more. You choose how to respond, the family reacts, and six meters move: Trust, Self, Parents, Standing, Money and Stress.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT
- A story that branches. How you handle one chapter decides which chapter comes next.
- Play solo with an AI partner, or online with your real partner using a room code, and see where your instincts differ.
- A private ledger. Every family member remembers how you treated them.
- Gossip that travels, wishes you can fulfil, and family treasures you can earn or lose.
- Turning points that trigger when stress or trust goes too far.
- Seven possible endings and a "story so far" map of your year.
- English and Urdu.
- A full house to explore: drag to look around, pinch to zoom.

WHO IT IS FOR
Engaged and newly married couples, and anyone curious about how a joint family really works. It is a safe place to practise hard conversations before they happen.

GOOD TO KNOW
- No account, no ads, no tracking.
- A feedback box inside the game goes straight to the team.
- The game is for learning. It is not a substitute for counselling, legal or religious advice.

A Dehleez project.
