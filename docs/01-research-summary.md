# 01. Research Summary

Compiled 5 November 2026 from web research. Findings are marked **[sourced]** (taken from a page read during research) or **[framework]** (established clinical or social-science framework, not re-verified here). Claims that need a deeper academic check before going into course scripts are listed at the bottom.

## 1. What the evidence says about South Asian marriages

### 1.1 Women-initiated divorce in Pakistan
- **[sourced]** A University of Karachi discussion reported almost 15,000 women filed for khula in Karachi in the previous year (reported as 14,970 in the headline and 14,973 in one speaker's remarks). Speakers cited financial instability as a main reason and excessive family interference as a frequent cause. Source: [Dawn, Oct 2024](https://www.dawn.com/news/1858837/over-14970-women-filed-for-khula-in-karachi-last-year-moot-told).
- **[sourced]** A qualitative study of 15 urban Lahore women who initiated divorce found long-term emotional neglect, recurring violence, rigid unequal gender roles, unaccountable husbands, in-law interference and mismatched marriages. Authors recommend premarital counselling and accessible support networks. Source: [Pakistan Social Sciences Review](https://ojs.pssr.org.pk/journal/article/view/1121). Small sample, urban, women only: treat as illustrative, not representative.

### 1.2 The unprepared transition
- **[sourced]** Couples focus on the wedding rather than the marriage. Finances, boundaries and shared goals are often undiscussed; cohabitation starts almost overnight because living together first is taboo; chores and roles are negotiated by friction; the bride is often expected to keep up a social calendar and a pleasant face; newcomers have to learn how relatives relate to each other and how much to share with them. Source: [5X Press](https://www.5xfest.com/5xpress/first-comes-love-then-comes-marriage-and-then-theres-the-part-no-one-talks-about).
- **[sourced]** Brides report being told the house is not theirs, being expected to adapt diet, spending and habits, serve the husband's relatives, and being met with a husband who settles in easily and never acknowledges the scale of change. Post-wedding low mood is linked to losing routines, family and home. Some commenters note husbands also face strain and the adjustment should be shared. Source: [Dawn Images](https://images.dawn.com/news/1189535).

### 1.3 Does premarital education work?
- **[sourced]** Meta-analysis of 47 coded studies (Fawcett, Hawkins, Blanchard, Carroll, 2010, *Family Relations*): programs improved couple communication (large effects when measured by observation, smaller by self-report); no clear improvement in relationship quality or satisfaction once unpublished studies were included; long-term follow-up studies are rare. Source: [BYU ScholarsArchive](https://scholarsarchive.byu.edu/facpub/4230).
- **Design consequence:** teach and rehearse communication behaviours, build follow-up into the product (month 1, 3, 6, 12 check-ins), and measure outcomes honestly.

### 1.4 In-law conflict research
- Searches surfaced studies on daughter-in-law and mother-in-law conflict in Korean and Chinese-Malaysian samples (for example on psychological wellbeing and marital adjustment). They support the broad pattern that mother-in-law discord and weak spousal support predict lower marital satisfaction, but they are not South Asian samples. **Action:** pull South Asian-specific studies (Pakistani Journal of Psychological Research, Indian family-studies journals) in the next research pass.

## 3. Legal and structural context (Pakistan first)

Needs review by a Pakistani family-law lawyer before any legal lesson is published.

- The **Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961** governs registration of marriage, restrictions on a second marriage without permission of an Arbitration Council, and talaq notice procedures. Overview: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim_Family_Laws_Ordinance,_1961); recent coverage of unlawful second marriages: [Nukta](https://nukta.com/guardians-of-consent-pakistans-courts-hold-husbands-accountable-for-unlawful-second-marriages).
- The **Nikahnama** has columns for mehr (dower), conditions, and whether the right of divorce is delegated to the wife. Couples rarely read or negotiate it. A "read your nikahnama" lesson is high value. (The Brandsynario explainer could not be fetched; use the Federal Shariat Court judgment and the form itself as primary sources.)
- Khula and the Dissolution of Muslim Marriages Act 1939 are the woman-initiated exit routes; they are covered descriptively in the "when it is not working" module.
